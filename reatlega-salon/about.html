About Us

The story behind the salon that celebrates you

Our Story

Reatlega Salon was born from a simple belief: every woman deserves to feel beautiful, confident, and celebrated. Founded in Gaborone, Botswana, we set out to create a space where quality beauty services meet genuine warmth and care.

The name Reatlega — meaning "we are pleased" in Setswana — says everything about our promise to you. We are not satisfied until you are. Every appointment, every style, every treatment is crafted with that commitment in mind.

From a single chair to a full-service salon, our journey has been shaped by the incredible women of Gaborone who trusted us with their beauty. We are proud to serve this community and to grow alongside it.
